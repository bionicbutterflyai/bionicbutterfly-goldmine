## ONE-LINE
Loud hook-skin at the top, sober quant-serious spine underneath — and the **gap between them is the
conversion event.** The guru-phobic skeptic clicks the loud hook braced for hype, hits the relief of
NOT getting hype, and converts on the contrast.

## THE MECHANISM
- **Hook-skin (top-of-funnel ONLY):** the Matrix/character hype aesthetic on TikTok thumbnails &
  cold-opens. Job: win the half-second scroll. It's a *wink* (obviously a costume) — reads as
  self-aware, not earnest-guru.
- **Sober spine (in-product):** the no-name voice-from-the-data, honest numbers (move≠bank), the Lab,
  flags its own engine's false negatives. The lock holds HERE.
- **EXTENDED this session:** the contrast doesn't stop at the click — the **whole almost-free foyer IS
  the sober-spine proving the loud hook wasn't lying.** Foyer quality = proof of the house. Anti-guru /
  "opposite-day": make it so good they chase you (earned standard, not fake scarcity).

## WHY IT CONVERTS THE HARDEST SEGMENT
Guru-phobic traders don't click clean-corporate (boring) OR trust hype (burned). The wink-hook stops
their scroll; the sober product earns the trust the costume couldn't. Target audience and brand voice
are in tension — the funnel turns the tension into the conversion.

## GUARDRAILS
1. **Fast hook→teaching handoff.** Character evaporates the instant teaching starts. Cold-open → hard
   cut → no-name voice. Lingering hype = the skeptic's "ugh, another guru" reflex fires.
2. **Hooks can't lie.** Real numbers + immediate move≠bank reframe. Hook and house tell the SAME true
   story at different volumes — not two stories.
3. **No hype tells post-click.** Chart, copy, voice, pricing all sober. One tell and the skeptic
   re-categorizes you as the thing they fled.

## VALIDATE
A/B Matrix-hook thumbnails vs clean ones on the same content. Measure CTR **and** post-click retention
— clicks that become the RIGHT students, not just clicks.
