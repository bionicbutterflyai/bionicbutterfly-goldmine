# GOLD — bioniq Q-LOGIC (the brand's operating logic + language)

## ONE-LINE
Not just a word list — the operating logic of the brand: how bioniq thinks, plus the Q-vocabulary that
falls out of it. Read the logic, then reach for the words. Everything keeps absorbing the letter Q.

## THE SPINE (the hero line)
> **Don't trade like a human. Execute like a machine.**
> **be bioniq**
Man vs. machine turned into a command. Humans are erratic/emotional; machines are disciplined/rule-bound;
*be bioniq* = a human with the machine's discipline (part man, part machine). Says the whole thesis in
seven words, zero explaining. This is the bar all other copy is judged against — most copy over-writes
because it doesn't trust a line this clean.

## QUBED (Q³) — the methodology
"Qubed" = Q-cubed = Q³ = the three Q's. A trade must pass **all three sides of the cube or the power stays
off** (ties to the Power-Q = power switch). Verb forms: **"Are you Qubed?" / "Get Qubed."**
- **Question** — what's the setup? (decision quality / structure present?)
- **Qualify** — what's the formula? (positive expected value vs. the historical journal?)
- **Quantify** — what's the risk? (RRR, position sizing, the stop)
Full blueprint: `reports/bioniq_execution_matrix.pdf`. (CHANGED FROM PRIOR: supersedes the plainer "3Q
framework" label — it's **Qubed / Q³** now.)

## THE GOOD-TRADE / BAD-TRADE INVERSION (core coaching principle)
A disciplined **loss** that followed the rules = a structural **win** (sound, repeatable). A **lucky win**
that broke the rules = a **hazard** (toxic conditioning; the math eventually liquidates it). Coach the
process, not the outcome. This is the honest-coach thesis and it drives the Bionic Lab's grading.

## THE Q-VOCABULARY (term — meaning)
- **bioniq** — the brand. part-human, part-machine. lowercase, Q not C.
- **be bioniq** — the mantra / tagline / sign-off.
- **the Power-Q** — the brandmark: a power symbol rotated 135° into a Q (one shape, two sizes: full for
  uppercase Q, scaled-down for lowercase q).
- **Qubed / Q³** — the methodology (above). "Get Qubed."
- **the 3 Q's — Question · Qualify · Quantify** — the cube's three sides.
- **Q-logic** — this doc: the brand's operating logic + language.
- **Q's = cues** — coaching cues (a WINK / spoken pun, e.g. "coaching Q's"). Not formal copy.
- **Coach Q's / "Coach Q"** — RESERVED future term (the coaching cues, and/or a coach role/persona). Hold.
- **Q'd = queued** — in the pipeline / next up (a WINK).
- **thanQs** — thank you, house-styled (a WINK / sign-off flourish).
- **Q&A** — the coaching conversation.
- **FAQ** — support/help.
- **QUANT / QUERY / QUALITY** — supporting Q-words (analytical DNA / every trade is a question / Q as a
  quality stamp). Wink coincidences (not pillars): QQQ (Nasdaq ETF), NQ (Nasdaq futures) — a private grin,
  never a public market-alignment claim.

## HOUSE STYLE
- The **name/logotype is lowercase `bioniq`** (q not C).
- The **capital-Q flourish (`bioniQ`, `thanQs`)** is reserved for taglines / sign-offs / winks — not the
  primary name. *(OPEN: Mark to confirm the exact capital-Q rule; provisional = lowercase name, capital-Q
  for flourishes.)*
- The **Qubed font** will make this moot in practice: a license-friendly base font (OFL or a EULA that
  explicitly permits modification — NEVER a hacked paid font) with **Q and q remapped to the Power-Q**
  (one shape, two sizes, size-matched to cap-/x-height). Then you type normally and the brand Q renders
  itself. (See brand_funnel_architecture.md.)
