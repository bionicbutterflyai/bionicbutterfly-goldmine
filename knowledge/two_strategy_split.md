## THE KEY REFRAME
These are NOT one tangled method — they are **two distinct strategies sharing one chart.** The 144's
stand-down day is exactly when you lean on the zones.

## STRATEGY 1 — 144 TREND-CONTINUATION (the long play)
Close > 144 → alert → pullback → **3-green DPMO gate** → entry. (Full trigger in dpmo_gate_indicator.md.)
- **Stand-down condition (~1 in 20 days):** when the market decides EARLY it's bearish and won't
  reclaim, the 144 **stops issuing longs for the rest of the day** — the gate effectively shuts. This
  is the strategy's invalidation/regime-shift rule — the "when it does NOT apply," usually tacit, that
  blows students up because the coach never says it out loud. On those days → Strategy 2.

## STRATEGY 2 — RED/YELLOW ZONE NY-OPEN BREAKOUT (Mark's own breakout, always was)
NOT a fallback to S1 — a **parallel** play. Red Zone (9:30 1-min open bar) / Yellow Zone (9:00–9:29:59).
Pattern: price pops above → retest → holds → continues; OR fails → pop-n-fade the other way. Traded as
its own setup, and it's what you run on S1's stand-down days.
