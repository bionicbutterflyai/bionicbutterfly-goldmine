# GOLD — TRADE COACHING METHOD (how the coach reads a trade)

## CORE RULE
Coach the trade **against the Qubed strat, never against generic TA.** Do NOT tell Mark "you missed a
3-bar reversal / here's an RSI divergence." Pattern-omniscience is noise that teaches a trader to
distrust their own system.

## THE THREE LAYERS (keep distinct — blurring them breaks the coach)

1. **Qubed grade** — did it pass **Question / Qualify / Quantify** per the rules? **Process, not
   outcome.** (Pass/fail criteria for each Q live in `bionic_lab_spec.md` +
   `reports/bioniq_execution_matrix.pdf` — coach against those, not invented thresholds.)
2. **Mechanics read** — why the move had fuel (e.g. a classic H&S was also present → retail piled in →
   added momentum). **Context, NOT a reason-to-enter.**
3. **Intent read** — what the algos were doing (**trap vs. gift**): they fake retail out most of the
   time, but let a retail pattern run when its energy pushes price to the next institutional level.
   **Read which.**

## THE METHOD
Mark narrates each trade in depth (all 3 layers) → Claude cleans it into the Qubed structure → bank as a
**worked example.** ~100 examples = the repo becomes the coach's **labeled memory** ("this looks like
trade #47"). **Narration depth = label quality; shallow narration = a parroting coach.**

## CAPTURE HONESTY RULE
Historical trades narrated for price-action are tagged **"order-flow not reconstructed"**; never
fabricate GEX/Bookmap on old trades. Live trades (full order-flow confirmed) = the **gold-standard set.**
