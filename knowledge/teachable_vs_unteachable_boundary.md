# GOLD — PRODUCT BOUNDARY: WHAT IS (AND ISN'T) TEACHABLE
*Protects the brand from promising the un-deliverable. This is a marketing + legal firewall, not just a
coaching note. RESTORED Chat 7 — it should never have left the vault.*

## SYSTEM ORIGIN (where the rules came from)
The Cubed rules are **reverse-engineered from Mark's own scalping.** Through 10,000+ hrs of reps his
pattern recognition got good enough to *feel* the spots live (overnight-high liquidity grabs, 144 retests,
level clusters, Bookmap stripes) and click in ~200ms. The scalping isn't a separate thing from the system —
it's the **raw, intuitive, unteachable version of it.** The platform is that same recognition **slowed down
and made explicit/rule-based**, so a student who lacks the reps can follow it: bigger moves, less size, edge
without the reflexes. **Mark's scalps are R&D** — each one demonstrates a pattern the coach should encode in
slowed-down, rule-based form.

## THE HARD BOUNDARY (proven, not theoretical)
Mark's scalping edge (reading Bookmap/GEX liquidity, millisecond reaction, no emotion) is **UNTEACHABLE** —
PROVEN by his inability to teach it to **Student Zero** (the first tester). Student Zero went green on the
**slowed-down, rule-based** system, NOT on the scalping. Therefore:
- The platform teaches the **TEACHABLE**: Levels · Timing · Behavior, rule-based confluence (Cubed),
  discipline, and ADHERENCE — **level-to-level**, not scalp.
- The platform must **NEVER claim to teach scalping / gut-feel / millisecond reaction.** Coaching scalping
  would **fail every student** (as it failed Student Zero), hand critics the *"even their own AI can't follow
  it"* attack, and is a risk-management hazard (scalping with size blows accounts).
- **Scalping = founder's PRIVATE method + R&D source for patterns. The RULE-SYSTEM = the transferable product.**

## THE FOUNDER'S SCALP METHOD (R&D — banked plainly; classic technique, not secret)
Mark's live method is **classic scalp discipline**, just less intense than pure HFT-style scalping:
- **Add to winners, NEVER to losers.** Probe small (2-3 micros), move to **B/E** fast for protection. Most
  often it scratches at B/E, or he bails the baby position down **$25-75**. **Never hold-and-hope.**
- When it **decides to go**, load up via Stream-Deck hotkey (~30 micros) and **ride a trailing stop** to the
  level. Average winning trade **$800-2,700** — the rare home-run pays for the string of scratches.
- He reframes scratches as **"business expenses, not losses."** *(Tripwire: this reframe is healthy ONLY
  while the scratches stay small/capped — the day a "business expense" becomes a $400 hold-and-hope, the
  language has flipped from discipline to denial. That flip is exactly what Layer-4 / the coach watches for.)*
- **The discipline it proves:** he learned to control the three killer thoughts — *"I'm wrong," "I'm due,"
  "it's just a little pullback."* That control is the whole game.
- **The tape rationale (why probe-and-scratch, not predict):** the level holds ~9/10 — but he doesn't know
  *when*, and the bots **sweep it first, lately sweeping DEEP then coming all the way back.** So the right
  play is **scratch-and-survive the sweep on baby size**, then add fast onto an in-the-money position while
  resetting B/E — not predict the exact bounce.
- **The adding mechanic (the teachable nugget):** adding moves your **average price closer to current
  price**, so a normal pullback stops you at B/E more often (protection). Refusing to add *and* waiting too
  long to add both cap a trader — wait too long and the pullback gaps your average and takes you out anyway.
- **Regime tag:** this edge is calibrated to the current **headline-driven, bot-and-tweet tape** (post-tariffs,
  Iran, NVDA/SpaceX). NOT a forever-claim — a low-vol regime may change it. Tag scalp-era trades accordingly.

## THE BRIDGE (why this matters to the taught product)
Scalp-discipline and rule-based level-to-level discipline are **two cures for the SAME disease** — the
"I'm wrong / I'm due / just a pullback" voice that sizes a trader into a loser (the psychological flaw in
coaching_philosophy.md's DIFFERENTIATION THESIS). Mark beat it through **scalp reps** (B/E-or-scratch, add
only to winners); Student Zero beats it through **rule-based patience** (wait for the alert + retest, hold
to the next level). Same principle — *never let the ego size you up into a loser* — at different intensities
and screen-time. The founder-method and the taught-product are not opposites; they're the **same discipline
on a spectrum** (scalp → level-to-level → swing).

## THE CONNECTION TO ACCOUNTABILITY
Students don't need Mark's **reflexes**; they need his **rules + the accountability to follow them.** The
coach teaches the rule-system and holds the discipline at the failure point. It never sells the gut-feel.
