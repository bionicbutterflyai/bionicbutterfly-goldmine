# PART A — THE BUSINESS THESIS

## ONE SENTENCE
We don't sell trading knowledge. We sell **accountability**. The knowledge is already free on every
guru's YouTube channel — what struggling traders lack, and will pay for, is something that holds them
to the plan.

## THE CORE INSIGHT
Most traders don't fail from ignorance. **The average trader can learn a strategy in a day but can't
trade it** — enters too early/late, moves stops, takes profit early, over-leverages, revenge-trades.
The problem is behavioral/psychological, not informational. A course that dumps more knowledge is
useless (they half-know it already). What changes outcomes is accountability + honest feedback against
their own logged behavior. That is the product. Everything else serves it.

## THE WEIGHT WATCHERS ANALOGY (the model)
Weight Watchers doesn't sell nutrition facts — everyone knows vegetables beat donuts. It sells the
**weigh-in**: structure, logging, accountability, showing up. We are Weight Watchers for traders. The
journal is the food diary; the coaching session is the weigh-in; the system makes you face your own
numbers. People don't pay for the calorie chart — they pay to step on the scale in front of someone
who cares whether they did the work. (Banked in coaching_philosophy.md.)

## WHO WE SERVE (and don't)
**ICP = capable-but-struggling traders** whose issue is psychology/discipline — some screen time, can
read a chart, keep blowing up on execution. Coachable, will pay for accountability. **NOT raw noobs**
(routed to nurture — future pipeline, not today's client). **NOT trolls** (filtered at the door by
real-identity/card). The funnel exists to sort these three and only escalate the ICP to a human.

## THE ARC: MARK-AS-COACH → MANY-COACHES (incl. the John Whiting sales-via-AI model)
- **Now:** Mark coaches 1:1 (Zoom, weekly). Has the time, no clients yet, LEARNS onboarding by living
  it. He IS the product at this stage. Records onboarding snippets over time → future self-onboarding.
- **Next:** the system (no-name voice + dossier + engine) carries more coaching, scaling past Mark's hours.
- **End:** **coach the coaches** — Mark trains coaches who run the playbook inside the platform; the
  system makes a coach consistent. Accountability at scale, Mark's method as the spine.
- BIG FORK (master_journey_flow.md, Fork 1): WHEN/HOW coaching de-personalizes without losing the trust 1:1 builds.
- **The John Whiting model (the sales-layer de-personalization blueprint).** Whiting is a YouTube
  influencer *to* YouTube influencers — he sells online businesses on killing the old
  appointment-setter → human-closer hard-sell and **replacing the human sales team with AI agents**:
  AI-driven drip gets prospects to binge the material and self-qualify, so you **filter FOR the clients
  you want** instead of chasing the ones you don't. That IS our AI funnel agent (drip · qualify) + the
  3-way behavioral sort + the anti-guru "make-them-chase-you" foyer — Whiting just gives it a concrete
  operating model and outside proof it works on this audience. **Now-vs-later:** today **Mark is the

  Full routing + closer decision tree (the three buckets; the course-as-hook-and-courtesy rule — never
  route a hot ICP through the 90%-off course, it cools the lead) → **funnel_routing_and_closer.md**.

## THE ANTI-GURU FUNNEL (full reasoning in funnel_brainstorm_reasoning.md)
Hype-skin hook (Matrix/Morphosis caricature, top-of-funnel ONLY) → sober quant-serious product. The
CONTRAST is the conversion: the guru-phobic skeptic clicks braced for hype, hits the relief of none,
converts. The almost-free foyer IS the sober-spine proving the hook didn't lie. "Opposite-day" — make
it so good they chase you. Course is double-duty: the hook AND the graceful "not yet" for the un-ready.
Boundary (lock carve-out): the caricature is hook-ONLY; the in-product voice stays unnamed.
**The $10 door is a FILTER, not revenue.** The almost-free course ($200 → $10) exists to collect a tiny
toll on a real credit card. The point isn't the $10 — it's that paying it makes you a **verified human
with a real identity and a real card (Amex).** Free attracts everyone, including trolls and
tire-kickers; a small-but-nonzero price repels the noise and proves minimal skin in the game. So the
course is the vehicle, the $10 charge is the velvet rope, and every verified payer becomes a known lead
— into the close if they're the ready ICP, into nurture if they're not yet. This is the "real card =
identity" foyer-door filter, now with its why. (Full door/routing detail → funnel_routing_and_closer.md.)

# PART B — THE PRODUCT-EXPERIENCE THESIS (why it feels like nothing else)
*(verified from the full Chat-4 transcript by Claude 4's deep-dive — the half a repo-only read misses)*

## THE NEW EYE vs THE BIONIC GLANCE (the problem the product solves)
Mark reads a chart in split-seconds — confluences "jump out," no thinking (the bionic gift). A new eye
(the student) can't. Mark's live TV chart with all ~14 strat levels on it is "a teenager's bedroom on a
Friday night." The entire product exists to bridge that gap: deliver Mark's instant read to an eye that
can't yet do it. **The moat isn't the levels (commodity) — it's the legibility.**

## FILL-ON-RELEVANCE (the governing display rule)
Every strat level/zone lives in the data layer ALWAYS, but the chart stays CLEAN by default — a level
only draws/fills when the coach cites it as confluence for that moment. New eye sees a clean chart and a
clear story; the confluence appears only when it matters. On-brand with "clean chart, none of the 7
commodity indicators." (See strat_zone_taxonomy.md display rules.)

## THE SUPER-SAUCE (the signature mechanic — narration-synced highlight-and-fade)
During the coach's voice presentation, as he *says* a level it **highlights, then fades back**. It's the
TEMPORAL form of fill-on-relevance: surface a level exactly when spoken, then let it recede. Bonus: the
free Web Speech API `onboundary` events can DRIVE the highlights in real time — the level lights as the
word is spoken. Free voice AND the narration-sync engine from one source. This is the delivery of the
whole legibility thesis. (Ties to voice_tts_decision.md.)

## THE MORPHING VIEWPORT + CONTINUITY (intake = playback, one persistent layout)
ONE persistent triptych: TV large-right, Bookmap upper-left, GEX lower-left. The student uploads her 3
charts into those spots; in the lesson the SAME panes stay put — the coach grows whichever chart he's
discussing to full-screen (others disappear for sharpness), then settles back. **The upload spot IS the
expand spot.** She never re-orients. Same room, same furniture — he just picks one up. Intake and
playback are ONE pipeline. The same narration cues fire both the highlight-and-fade AND the morph.

## THE BOOKMAP/GEX SOCRATIC LOOP (accountability honesty in action)
During a trade eval the coach asks: *would Bookmap liquidity / GEX gamma have helped here?* If yes →
Socratic ("what in the Bookmap supports staying in toward that level?") — draws the student's read out.
**If nothing on those charts would have changed the decision → say so honestly** ("nothing here you
needed — the gate and the levels carried it"). That honesty IS the coaching integrity — the same
anti-hype spine as the brand. (Pending CUE #3.)

## THE IMAGE-ROLE GUARDRAIL (why we redraw from raw data)
Bookmap & TanukiTrades GEX are CONSISTENT surfaces — every student's looks the same → Claude can read
them reliably. TradingView is NOT — every student's TV is a different mess. So: **analyze Bookmap & GEX;
NEVER parse the student's TV** (display-reference only). The strat is rebuilt from RAW trade data in our
own system — which is exactly what protects it from every student's messy/incomplete TV. Not just a
build choice: it's what makes the strat deliverable to anyone regardless of how they chart.
(See data_provenance_and_timestamp_pin.md, strat_zone_taxonomy.md.)

# PART C — THE BUILD & THE MONEY (Chat-5; the how)

## THE COACH'S FORM (locked)
Voice-first — the coach speaks back, because texting is a chatbot and quizzes are a 2002 website. **No
name, no image, no avatar** — just the voice from the data. The lip-sync **avatar was KILLED on cost**
(HeyGen/D-ID ~10x the coaching-call itself). Voice ships free on the browser-native **Web Speech API**
($0/student); paid premium voice is optional/later. The engine is credited as **"powered by Claude"**
(the partner-standard phrasing) — never "Claude Coach" as a name. (See coaching_philosophy.md, voice_tts_decision.md.)

## EFFECTIVE LEARNING — the Lisa call (and what it is NOT)
Lisa (Mark's wife), a corporate online-learning professional at Volvo Learning North America, made the
blunt call: the **2002 model — read copy, answer questions, next chapter — is dead** (boring,
ineffective). Our design principles follow from *effective* online learning: **interactive ·
personalized · engaging · gamified · explain/show-WHY** (not multiple choice). **What this is NOT:** we
are not Volvo and do not claim its enterprise L&D apparatus (risk/legal sign-off, formal
ROI-on-learning metrics) — we inherit the effectiveness principle, not a corporate-standards pedigree.
**Validation:** she's seen the live demo, and her reaction was **"you can really do that!"** — a rare
bar, cleared. (See learning_design_standards.md. Note: a repo-only Claude has NOT seen the demo —
treat product-feel as reported, not observed.)

## THE MONEY (the credit engine)
Not a flat AI subscription — a credit loop: base credits with the course, every AI/coaching interaction
consumes credits, top up via Stripe when they run out (Redis rate-limits abuse), balance in the Supabase
RLS profile. **The credit engine IS the per-student cost model** — curiosity monetized without capping
engagement.

## HOW IT'S WIRED (one line per layer)
Users → Netlify frontend (landing, auth, student "Cockpit", Incubator/Accelerator course player, admin
"Mothership") → Supabase backend (Postgres+RLS, dossier + credit ledger, **Edge Functions = API proxy +
verified-data gate**, realtime live room) → external services (Anthropic = the coach, **Massive.com**
= the v2 datafeed [v1 = manual exports; ex-Polygon, free 5/min delayed, CME futures incl. micros],
Stripe = payments/credits, Discord = paid live room) → GitHub→CI/CD→Netlify devops. Two flows ride on
top: the **credit engine** and the **Fork-2 memory loop** (foyer behavior → dossier → the coach reads
it = "we already know you"). (See tech_architecture_skeleton.md + tech_architecture_master.svg.)

## WHY THIS REPO EXISTS (so no Claude re-learns Weight Watchers)
The hardest recurring cost was never building — it was the handoff. Concepts died each chat because the
*why* lived only in conversation. Repo-as-memory is the fix: write the why back so each new Claude
inherits it from README → this doc, not from the founder's fingers. Explaining a thing is full-time work
only until it's banked; after that it's a one-time cost, forever. (See repo_as_memory_and_handoff.md.)

## LOCKS (full list in master_journey_flow.md)
No named entity / no guru character (the voice IS the platform — it sees your data, doesn't predict your
future); no avatar (killed on cost). Lab = scientist's bench, not trading pit. Hype-skin is
top-of-funnel ONLY (the lock is product-side). ICP = capable-but-struggling, not noobs. Accountability
is the product; knowledge is free bait. Clean chart by default; levels fill on relevance. Attribution =
"powered by Claude," not a coach name.
