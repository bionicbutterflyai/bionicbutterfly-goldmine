## TWO SIMPLE RULES
1. **One file, one folder** — pick the best fit above; don't duplicate across folders.
2. **Heavy media (mp4, large audio) = a LINK, not a file** — list it in `_media-links.txt` (name +
   link + one line). Keeps the zip light to transfer.
